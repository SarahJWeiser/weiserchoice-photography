Welcome to Weiser Choice Photography

At Weiser Choice Photography, we believe every story deserves to be seen, celebrated, and remembered. We are a proud LGBTQ+- and woman-owned, family-owned and operated photography business built on creativity, compassion, and a genuine love for capturing life's most meaningful moments. Before pursuing photography professionally, I spent 10 years serving my community as a firefighter and EMT. Those years taught me the importance of compassion, connection, trust, and appreciating the moments that matter most. I bring that same dedication and attention to detail to every photography session. My passion for storytelling led me to study photography in college, with a focus in photojournalism. That background shaped the way I approach my work: looking beyond the posed picture to capture the authentic emotions, candid interactions, and little details that make every story unique. As a family-owned and operated business, we understand how important family, relationships, and memories are. We strive to create a welcoming, comfortable, and inclusive experience for everyone who steps in front of our camera. Whether we're documenting a wedding, celebrating a new arrival, capturing family portraits, or preserving a special event, our goal is simple: to create photographs you'll treasure for a lifetime. Your story matters. Let us help you tell it.
